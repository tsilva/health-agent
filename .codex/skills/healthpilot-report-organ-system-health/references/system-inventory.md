## Mapping rules

- Score an organ in both functional contexts only when appropriate; for example, endocrine and exocrine pancreas are distinct functions.
- Put anemia directly under red blood cells, then note its downstream effects without penalizing every other system as if each had primary disease.
- Put obesity and general lifestyle risks in endocrine/metabolic or cross-system context; do not treat them as direct structural disease everywhere.
- Add organs such as spleen, prostate, uterus, ovaries, testes, or specific valves as extra rows when direct evidence supports separate scoring.
